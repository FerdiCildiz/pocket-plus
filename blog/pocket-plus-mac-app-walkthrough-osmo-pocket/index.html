Pocket+ for Mac:
a full walkthrough

Download Pocket+Free for DJI Osmo Pocket 2, 3, 4 and 4P · macOS 15 or later

If you create content at a desk, the DJI Osmo Pocket can be awkward: a tiny screen, a joystick you keep reaching for, and a connection in DJI Mimo that drops or stutters. I'm a user experience designer, so I built Pocket+, a Mac app that gives the Pocket a proper big-screen home. This post follows the walkthrough video, feature by feature.

What Pocket+ does

Connect your Osmo Pocket to the Mac with USB, and Pocket+ gives you a live preview, gimbal control and recording straight to your Mac. The free version already records 1080p directly to the Mac, so you can start without paying anything. It works with the Osmo Pocket 3, and the video also shows the Pocket 4.

Recording controls

Recording to the Mac comes with the controls you'd expect: start, pause, finish and delete. You can also change the recording resolution between 1080p at 30 fps and 4K at 30 fps (4K is a Pro feature). Pocket+ can take photos too, with an optional countdown timer.

The interface: audio meter and histogram

The main window shows an audio level meter and a histogram (Luma or RGB), so you can check your sound and exposure at a glance instead of guessing from a small camera screen.

Gimbal control

  • Joystick on screen to frame your shot, with fine adjustment for small moves.
  • Zoom and keyboard shortcuts so your hands can stay on the keys.
  • Drag control (Pro) turns the whole preview into a joystick: drag to move the gimbal and use the scroll wheel to zoom.

Reframe and Auto Framing

Reframe finds your face and centers it automatically. Face tracking follows you as you move, and Auto Framing keeps you nicely composed without touching anything.

Framing guides

Turn on a vertical 9:16 guide, a grid or a horizon line. The 9:16 guide is handy when you are shooting for Reels, Shorts or TikTok but still want the camera in its normal landscape position.

Wide-angle lens correction

With the 0.75x wide-angle lens, the picture can bulge at the edges. Pocket+ has a lens correction option that straightens it out.

Adjust panel and LUTs

The Adjust panel gives you live control over contrast, shadows, highlights and saturation. On top of that you can pick a LUT look: Cinematic, Cool, Faded, Warm, Mono or Vivid. The color settings are applied to your recordings automatically, not just the preview.

Scenes: switch framing with Alt+1 to Alt+9

Save up to nine framings as scenes, then jump between them with a single shortcut, Alt+1 through Alt+9. It's a quick way to cut from a wide shot to a close-up on a one-person set.

Vertical video without rotating the camera

Vertical mode records about 2.7K vertical video while the camera stays in its normal landscape position. No rotating the gimbal, no re-framing your whole setup.

Record on the camera and the Mac at the same time

Press the camera's own record button and Pocket+ follows it, recording on both the camera and the Mac at once. In the video's example, the camera records 4K at 60 fps and the Mac records 4K at 30 fps, so you get a high-quality original plus a ready-to-use copy.

Virtual camera for Meet, OBS and more

Pocket+ includes a virtual camera, so you can use the Pocket as a webcam in Google Meet, OBS and other apps, with your framing, face tracking and color look applied.

Settings and small touches

  • Choose the recording codec and the countdown length.
  • Set the gimbal's center position.
  • Control the app from the menu bar.
  • Keep the window always on top.

Free and Pro

The free version covers live preview and 1080p recording directly to your Mac. Pro adds features like 4K 30 fps recording and the drag-to-control joystick. You can try Pocket+ for free and upgrade only if you need the extras.
