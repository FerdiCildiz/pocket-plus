Is there a DJI Mimo
app for Mac?

Download Pocket+Free for DJI Osmo Pocket 2, 3, 4 and 4P · macOS 14 or later

Short answer: no. DJI Mimo, the companion app for the Osmo Pocket, is made for iPhone, iPad and Android. DJI doesn't make a Mac version, so there is no Mimo for macOS, MacBook, iMac or Mac mini. You can still control a Pocket from a Mac, though, just over a cable instead of Bluetooth.

What about the iPad version on a Mac?

Apple Silicon Macs can install some iPad apps from the App Store. The Mimo listing shows the iPad version as available on Macs with an M1 chip or later, but marks it "Not verified for macOS". That means DJI hasn't tested or tuned it for the Mac. We haven't tested whether it connects to a Pocket from a Mac either, so treat it as a maybe, not a solution.

What Mimo does

On a phone, Mimo connects to the Pocket over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. It gives you:

  • a live view and remote control of the camera,
  • file transfer from the camera to the phone,
  • video editing: trimming, splitting, speed and more,
  • firmware updates, which Mimo offers when a new version is out.

The best DJI Mimo alternative for Mac

Plug the Pocket into the Mac with a USB-C cable and choose Webcam on the camera's screen. The Mac then sees a standard webcam. That's enough for FaceTime, Zoom or OBS, but macOS has no way to move the gimbal or zoom. The camera does accept those commands over USB, so an app can send them.

That's what Pocket+ does. It's a free Mac app for the Pocket 2, 3, 4 and 4P that gives you:

  • a live preview, photos and recording straight to the Mac,
  • an on-screen gimbal joystick, plus center and flip,
  • zoom with buttons or keyboard shortcuts,
  • face tracking that turns the gimbal to follow you.

An optional Pro upgrade adds 4K recording, LUTs, auto framing, saved camera angles and vertical video. While you stream or call from OBS, Zoom or Google Meet, you can keep steering the camera in Pocket+.

For footage that's already on the camera's card, the simplest route on a Mac is a microSD card reader. If you shoot in D-Log M, DJI offers official LUTs for the Pocket 3 on its downloads page, which work in Final Cut Pro or DaVinci Resolve.

When you still need Mimo

Pocket+ doesn't replace Mimo, it covers the desk. Keep Mimo on your phone for firmware updates, for shooting away from the computer and for quick edits. When the Pocket sits next to your Mac as a camera for calls, streams or recordings, Pocket+ gives you the controls Mimo would give you on a phone.

Quick answers

Is there a Mimo app for macOS?

No. DJI makes Mimo for iPhone, iPad and Android only. The iPad version can be installed on Apple Silicon Macs, but DJI hasn't verified it for macOS.

Does Mimo work on a MacBook?

Not officially. If you want to use the Pocket with a MacBook, plug it in over USB-C in Webcam mode and use an app like Pocket+ to steer the gimbal and zoom.

Can I control an Osmo Pocket 3 from a Mac?

Yes. Over USB-C the camera accepts gimbal and zoom commands, and Pocket+ sends them from the Mac. Live view, photos and recording to the Mac work too.
