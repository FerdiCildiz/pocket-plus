What's new in Pocket+

What is new in each version of Pocket+. The app tells you when an update is ready and installs it in one click.

2.0 November 11, 2026

  • Compact view and menu bar (Pro): a small live camera with record, photo, face tracking, auto frame and the record countdown. Pin it floating, or keep Pocket+ in the menu bar with a record timer
  • Virtual camera (Pro): use the Pocket in Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, WhatsApp, OBS and Chrome through the Pocket+ Camera, at 1080p or 4K
  • A new first-run: a short welcome that asks for the camera, microphone and Bluetooth with clear Grant buttons, then continues where you left off if you leave in the middle. If Pocket+ runs from Downloads, drag its icon to Applications so one-click updates work
  • Settings is now a window with pages: Recording, Gimbal, Window, License, Updates, About and Diagnostics
  • Adjust is Pro: Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Temperature, Tint, Saturation and Vibrance, with Auto and Hold to compare
  • Zoom: hold ⌥ and click + or − (or turn the mouse wheel) for 0.1× steps. The zoom bar follows the camera's own zoom, also while you use the joystick
  • Resolutions above 1080p, vertical video and the compact view show a crown while they are locked. Smooth gimbal movement is always on
  • Recording: pause, restart or delete a take while you record. Start a recording from the camera's button and Pocket+ shows a red 3 second countdown, and stopping only the Mac recording asks first
  • Quit asks first, and while you record Pocket+ will not quit until you stop
  • Selfie flip: the joystick, arrows and dragging no longer turn backwards after Front/Back or when the camera opens. Pocket+ measures the direction from the picture itself, and Reset camera measures it again
  • Recordings now look like the preview and the photos; before, videos came out a little brighter (macOS 15 and later)
  • Photo countdown, plus camera sounds for the shutter and for starting and stopping a recording
  • Windows open on your main screen, and Pocket+ remembers where you leave the main one
  • Photos and videos are named PocketPlus and the date, like PocketPlus091125
  • Fixed a crash in the virtual camera and broken pictures that could show up when switching to vertical
  • Built for macOS 15 or later (macOS 14 is not tested). Pocket+ has not been tested on Intel Macs

1.7 November 2, 2026

  • Adjust: a new panel next to Wide Angle with Exposure, Contrast, Highlights and Shadows (Pro) and Temperature, Tint, Saturation and Vibrance. It applies to the preview, recordings and photos, and Hold to compare shows the original while you press
  • Auto adjust: one click sets the sliders for the scene, and leaves good pictures alone
  • Histogram: open it from the top left under the microphone; click it to switch between brightness and red, green and blue
  • 8 languages: English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português, 日本語 and 简体中文, with a Language setting in Settings › Window
  • A new opening screen, and the window now opens centered the first time and remembers where you leave it
  • Move to Applications: if Pocket+ runs from Downloads, it offers to move itself to Applications so one-click updates work
  • A short startup effect when the first picture arrives (turn it off in Settings › Window)
  • Scenes: update a scene to the current view with one click; the scene you are on stays yellow and the others dim
  • Returning to the last position now glides instead of snapping
  • Coming back to the window after working in another app no longer looks like a fresh start, and Settings › Window › Pause when hidden lets you turn the pause off
  • Clicking another button while a panel is open now works with a single click
  • Auto frame sits next to Record and face tracking moved under the joystick
  • Track everyone (Settings › Gimbal, off by default): face tracking follows everyone in the frame; double-click a face to follow just one person
  • Build 23 (2026-11-02): photos now have the same colors as the preview instead of looking washed out
  • Build 24 (2026-11-03): a new app icon

1.6 November 1, 2026

  • Pro now comes as a one-time Lifetime license or a yearly plan, bought through Polar. Licenses you already have keep working as they are
  • Settings › Pro › Remove from this Mac frees a license so you can activate it on another Mac
  • Locked features now show a small crown instead of the PRO label

1.5.1 October 23, 2026

  • Fixed left and right going the wrong way after switching between the front and back of the camera (seen on Osmo Pocket 4): the direction is now learned separately for each side

1.5 October 20, 2026

  • Vertical video (9:16, Pro): switch with the buttons at the top or ⇧⌘V, or just turn the Pocket upright. True vertical with the Pocket upright, or a sharp 1080×1920 crop from 4K without turning the camera
  • Wide-angle lens correction (Pro): the new Wide Angle button straightens the curved edges of the DJI wide-angle lens, with a strength slider, keeping people at the edges natural
  • Return to last position (Pro): when the camera reconnects it turns and zooms back to where you left it; landscape and vertical keep their own positions and scenes
  • Reset camera position from the title bar: centers the camera at 1× and forgets the last position
  • Deleting a scene asks for confirmation right next to it (Delete ✓ ✕)
  • The preview no longer freezes when OBS, Google Meet or Zoom opens the camera
  • Auto framing in vertical frames a talking-head shot
  • Remembers resolution, frame rate and codec
  • ⌘S saves a scene (photo is now ⇧⌘S); the mouse wheel zooms over the zoom control and joystick
  • Correct zoom limits for Osmo Pocket 4 and 4P
  • Only the Osmo Pocket connects automatically, with a hint when it isn't found
  • The preview shows the same colors as recordings
  • New look: SF Symbols icons, redesigned Settings, smooth transitions between camera modes
  • The window opens on the display where you left it
  • A short guided tour shows how to aim by dragging the picture and zoom by scrolling (Pro)
  • The resolution button shows the right icon for 4K, 2.7K, HD or vertical
  • Build 19 (2026-10-21): a little confetti celebration when you activate Pro in the app
  • Build 18 (2026-10-21): future-proof signing, so updates and your Pro license keep working when the signing certificate is renewed

1.1.5 October 20, 2026

  • The Osmo Pocket is always picked first, and virtual cameras (Camo, OBS and similar) no longer show up in the camera list
  • Your Pro license is now stored securely in the macOS Keychain and tied to this Mac
  • Pro confirms your license online now and then; it keeps working offline for up to 30 days
  • Settings › Pro shows the license status and lets you check again

1.1.4 October 20, 2026

  • Scenes now live on the picture: up to 9 numbered buttons at the top right, one click to go there (or ⌥1–9)
  • Hover a scene to see its name, with rename and delete right next to it
  • Save a scene with the + button and give it a name
  • New PRO badge

1.1.3 October 20, 2026

  • The camera stream pauses while the window is minimized or hidden, so the camera stays cooler
  • More reliable correction when the gimbal turns the wrong way during face tracking (Osmo Pocket 4)
  • Updates can now also arrive as improved builds of the same version
  • New app icon

1.1.2 October 19, 2026

  • Face tracking corrects itself when the gimbal turns the wrong way (Osmo Pocket 4); joystick and dragging follow
  • The camera stream pauses while the window is minimized or hidden, so the camera stays cooler
  • More reliable direction detection on cameras with a slower gimbal

1.1.1 October 19, 2026

  • In-app updates: the app checks GitHub once a day and installs new versions in one click (Settings › Updates)
  • A clear prompt when the camera's own ActiveTrack takes over the gimbal, with one-click resume
  • Face tracking no longer switches itself off when you move from side to side

1.1 October 19, 2026

  • Pro licenses are now available on Gumroad: buy, paste the key in Settings › Pro, done
  • One license activates Pro on one Mac (re-activating on the same Mac is always fine)
  • Fixed the window jumping and flickering when switching between 1080p and 4K

1.0 October 19, 2026

  • Live preview, recording (H.264 / HEVC) and photos from the DJI Osmo Pocket 3
  • Gimbal joystick, center, front/back; saved scenes (Pro)
  • Face tracking; auto framing (Pro)
  • Color looks with built-in, `.cube` and PNG LUTs (Pro)
  • Grid, horizon and 9:16 (Pro) guides
  • Zoom and aim by scrolling or dragging on the preview (Pro)
  • DJI Mic auto-switch, audio meter, record countdown, battery and disk info
  • 4K recording (Pro); free version records up to 1080p
