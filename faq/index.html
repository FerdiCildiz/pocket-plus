Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about setup, compatibility, recording and getting the most out of Pocket+.

Cameras & Compatibility

Which cameras are supported?

Pocket+ supports DJI Osmo Pocket 2, 3, 4 and 4P over USB-C in Webcam mode. Most development and testing is done on the Pocket 3. Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later on Apple Silicon or Intel.

Can I use my Osmo Pocket 3 as a webcam on Mac?

Yes. Connect it with USB-C, select Webcam mode and open Pocket+. View the live feed, control the gimbal and zoom, enable face tracking and record to your Mac.

Is this the DJI Mimo app for Mac?

No. DJI Mimo is DJI's phone app, and there's no Mac version of it. Pocket+ is an independent Mac app that gives you a live view, gimbal and zoom control over USB-C, and recording to your Mac. Keep Mimo on your phone for firmware updates.

Is Pocket+ affiliated with DJI?

No. Pocket+ is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by DJI.

Why only 4K and 1080p at 30, 25 and 24 fps?

Those are the modes the camera offers over USB in Webcam mode. 4K60 and 1080p60 aren't available over USB.

Can I use it wirelessly?

Not yet. Pocket+ works over a USB-C cable.

Setup & Permissions

Is Pocket+ notarized by Apple?

Yes. Pocket+ is signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens without the usual unidentified-developer warning. Its network requests are limited to update and license checks.

Why does it ask for camera, microphone and Bluetooth access?

Camera and microphone are for the picture and sound. Bluetooth reads the camera's battery, USB state and record button. Nothing leaves your Mac.

How do updates work?

Pocket+ checks for a new version once a day. When there is one, click Install and relaunch. You can turn the check off in Settings › Updates.

Why can't I see a camera image?

Check that your Pocket is in Webcam mode. Then click Reconnect at the top right of Pocket+. If the image still doesn't appear, unplug and reconnect the USB-C cable.

Gimbal & Tracking

Why does face tracking switch off?

The camera's own ActiveTrack may be enabled. Disable ActiveTrack on the camera, then enable face tracking in Pocket+ again.

Is face tracking the camera's ActiveTrack?

No. Pocket+ detects your face on your Mac and physically moves the gimbal; it isn't a digital crop. It's off when the app opens; switch it on with the button below the joystick or with ⌘T. It's free.

What is auto framing?

One click centers everyone in view and adjusts the zoom to fit them, once, then stops. It doesn't keep moving like face tracking. Auto framing is a Pro feature.

Can I save gimbal positions?

Yes. Scenes save up to 9 camera angles you can recall with one click or ⌥1 to ⌥9. Pro can also return the gimbal to its last position when you reopen the app. Both are Pro features.

How can I control the gimbal?

With the on-screen joystick, the arrow keys and shortcuts. Pro adds dragging the picture to steer and scrolling to zoom.

Recording & Audio

Where do my recordings go?

Into your Movies folder. You can pick another folder in Settings › Output folder.

Can I record in 4K?

4K is a Pro feature. The free version records up to 1080p. You can choose H.264 or HEVC in Settings.

Can I use a DJI Mic or another microphone?

Yes. A DJI Mic paired with the camera shows up as "DJI Mic". A DJI Mic receiver plugged into the Mac is picked automatically. You can also choose any Mac microphone from the mic menu.

Do LUTs apply to my recordings?

Your choice. The "Apply LUT to recordings" switch in the LUT menu decides it: off means the LUT is only for your preview. LUTs are a Pro feature.

Pro & Licensing

Is Pocket+ free?

Yes. Photos, recording up to 1080p, joystick control, zoom and face tracking are free. Pro adds up to 4K recording, LUTs, scenes and auto framing. Choose $39.99 one-time or $14.99/year. No account needed.

How do I activate Pro?

Buy Pro and you'll get a license key by email. Paste it into Settings › Pro. Each license activates Pro on one Mac. To use it on another Mac, choose Remove from this Mac in Settings › Pro first.

Other Apps

Does it work with OBS and Google Meet?

Yes. While OBS, Google Meet or WhatsApp uses the camera, Pocket+ can still steer the gimbal and zoom. Those apps receive the camera feed directly; LUTs and lens correction aren't passed through without a virtual camera.
